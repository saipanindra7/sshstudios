Privacy notice.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This notice explains how SH Studio, founded by Saipanindra and Hemanth and based in India, handles information visitors choose to share when contacting us through this website.

Information you choose to provide

The project enquiry form asks for your name, email address, the type of service you are interested in and a description of your project. Phone number and preferred timeline are optional. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

How the enquiry form works

This website is a static site. When you submit the enquiry form, the details are placed into a pre-filled WhatsApp message draft addressed to SH Studio. Your browser opens WhatsApp; the message is not delivered to us unless you review it and press Send. If WhatsApp does not open, you can contact us directly by email. Choosing the email link opens a draft in your email application; it is not sent until you press Send.

Third-party services

If you choose to send a message, WhatsApp or your email provider will process it under its own terms and privacy notice. This website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. The hosting provider may also process basic technical request information in server logs according to its own policies. SH Studio has not intentionally added advertising pixels or analytics tracking to this website.

Storage and retention

The website itself does not submit enquiry form entries to a SH Studio database or store them on this site. Once you send a WhatsApp or email message, the information is handled by the relevant messaging or email service and may remain in those services and SH Studio's business correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond and manage the enquiry.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit the form, remove information from the draft before sending, or contact us directly. For questions about an enquiry you have sent to us, email saipanindra1234@gmail.com.
