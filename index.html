06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Curious?
We get it.

You don’t need to know the technical details. Ask us what’s possible — we’ll help you figure out a practical way forward.

How long does it take to build a website? +

A simple landing page may take a few days once content is ready. Multi-page websites and stores can take longer. We'll agree on a timeline before starting.

Will my website work on mobile? +

Yes. We design for phones, tablets, and desktops and test responsive layouts before delivery.

Do I need to buy a domain or hosting? +

Most websites need a domain and a hosting arrangement. We'll explain your options; these services may be charged separately.

Can I update the website later? +

That depends on the type of site. We can discuss an easy-to-edit content management system or provide guidance for basic changes.

Do you work with clients outside India? +

Yes, we can discuss remote projects with clients in other countries. Currency, scope, and payment milestones are agreed before work begins.
